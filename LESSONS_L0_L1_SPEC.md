### L0.01 - What is Trading?

**Title (EN)**: What is Trading?
**Title (RW - DRAFT)**: Trading ni iki?

**Content (EN)**:
Trading means buying and selling things to make a profit. In trading, you buy something at a low price and sell it at a higher price. The difference is your profit. For example, if you buy gold at $1,800 and sell it at $1,850, you make $50 profit. But prices can also go down, so you can lose money too. That is why we practice first in a sandbox.

**Content (RW - DRAFT)**:
Trading ni ugukura no kugurisha ibintu kugira ngo utange umusaruro. Muri trading, ugura ikintu ku bw'iciro rito ukagurisha ku bw'iciro rinini. Impuzandengo ni umusaruro wawe. Urugero, iyo ugura zahabu ku $1,800 ukagurisha ku $1,850, utanga $50 umusaruro. Ariko ibiciro birashobora nanone kugwa, bityo ushobora gucagura. Niyo mpamuru dutangira gukorera imyitozo muri sandbox.

### L0.03 - How to Spot Trading Scams

**Title (EN)**: How to Spot Trading Scams
**Title (RW - DRAFT)**: Uko Wandika Imbugabiraza Z'abatanga Imyitwarire

**Content (EN)**:
Many scams promise you will get rich quick in trading. They say "guaranteed profit" or "no risk". This is never true. Trading always has risk. Real trading education teaches you about risk first. Scams often ask you to send money to them directly. Real brokers are licensed and regulated. Never give your password or private keys to anyone. If it sounds too good to be true, it is probably a scam.

**Content (RW - DRAFT)**:
Imbugabiraza benshi bavuga ko uzaba umusangiye bwite muri trading. Bavuga "umusaruro w'ihuriro" cyangwa "nta rwa risk". Ibi ntibwo binyuranye. Trading ifite risk zose gihe. Uburezi bwa trading bukubije ku risk mbere. Imbugabirira benshi bakubwira kohereza amafaranga ku bo bwite. Abafata imyitwarire b'ukwiciwe bafite uruhushya na baturutse mu biganiro. Utagaragaza ijambo ry'ibanga cyangwa urufunguzo rwawe ku muntu uwo ari we wese. Iyo bivugwa ko ari biiza cyane bitarenze uko bivuze, birashobora kuba imbugabirira.

### L1.01 - What is FOREX?

**Title (EN)**: What is FOREX?
**Title (RW - DRAFT)**: FOREX ni iki?

**Content (EN)**:
FOREX means foreign exchange. It is the market where people trade currencies. For example, you can trade the Euro against the US Dollar (EUR/USD). ### L1.03 - What is a Pip?

**Title (EN)**: What is a Pip?
**Title (RW - DRAFT)**: Pip ni iki?

**Content (EN)**:
A <Term term="pip">pip</Term> is the smallest price move in FOREX. For most pairs like EUR/USD, a pip is 0.0001. For USD/JPY, a pip is 0.01. For gold (XAU/USD), a pip is 0.01. Pips measure how much the price moved. If EUR/USD moves from 1.1000 to 1.1005, it moved 5 pips. Pips are important because your profit or loss is calculated in pips.

**Content (RW - DRAFT)**:
<Term term="pip">Pip</Term> ni imyitozo ndogo ya mbere muri FOREX. Kuri pairs zenshi nka EUR/USD, pip ni 0.0001. Kuri USD/JPY, pip ni 0.01. Kuri zahabu (XAU/USD), pip ni 0.01. Pips zigaragaza icuro ryahindutse iki. Iyo EUR/USD yahindutse uva kuri 1.1000 kugeza kuri 1.1005, yahindutse pips 5. Pips zingirwa kubera umusaruro cyangwa ucagawe ugaragazwa muri pips.

A <Term term="lot">lot</Term> is the size of your trade. A standard lot is 100,000 units of the base currency. A mini lot is 10,000 units. A micro lot is 1,000 units. Abato bagomba gutangira na micro lots cyangwa nto. Ingano ya lot ikomara neza pip buri wose irimo umusaruro cyangwa ucagawe buri pip. Hitamoje ingano ya lot ihuye na risk yawe.
